# INCAR Business Foundation

## Positioning

INCAR AUTO PARTS is a specialized B2B auto parts supplier serving wholesale buyers and importers across the Middle East from China.

Core positioning: Specialized Auto Parts Supplier from China for the Middle East.

## Target Customer

The primary customer is a Middle Eastern wholesale auto parts buyer, distributor, importer, garage supplier, or purchasing team that needs a specialized supply partner with knowledge of parts, manufacturing, specifications, inspection, packaging, and the China market.

## Business Model

The customer deals commercially with INCAR. INCAR:

- Reviews part, OEM reference, application, and specification requirements.
- Selects factories and manufacturing sources internally.
- Performs pricing analysis and issues quotations under the INCAR name.
- Manages agreed production, inspection, quality-control, packaging, and supply follow-up.
- Keeps factories backstage rather than presenting them as customer-facing choices.

INCAR is not a broker, translator, factory directory, marketplace, connection platform, or temporary agent that exits the supply relationship.

## RFQ-First Rule

The website should guide serious buyers toward preparing a clear RFQ. Products are not retail items for instant purchase; they are starting points for reference review, MOQ clarification, and later INCAR quotation preparation. Upload and submission are not active.

## No Ecommerce Checkout Rule

INCAR must not behave like a retail ecommerce store. The site should not add checkout, carts, shopping bags, retail order placement, payment flows, or buy-now behavior.

## No Payment Rule

The website must not collect payment or present payment as a website workflow. Commercial terms should be handled later through direct B2B communication after RFQ review.

## Private Label Core Feature

Private Label is a core supply capability. The site should show that INCAR manages product and specification requirements, manufacturing-source selection, custom packaging, logo and label information, samples when agreed, production, inspection checkpoints, and supply follow-up.

## Catalogs as Lead Generation

Catalogs must be INCAR-approved product catalogs, not factory listings or manufacturing-source libraries. No catalog request submission is active. Future approved catalogs may include product photos, part numbers, OEM numbers, MOQ, specifications, compatibility notes, and Private Label information.

## Trust-First Website Strategy

Every page should answer: why should a Middle Eastern wholesale buyer trust INCAR and send an inquiry?

The trust strategy should emphasize INCAR responsibility with careful, evidence-aware language:

- We review requirements.
- We select manufacturing sources internally.
- We issue the INCAR quotation.
- We manage agreed inspection and packaging checkpoints.
- We follow the approved supply requirements.

The website should avoid unverified claims such as guaranteed quality, certified factories, lowest price, biggest supplier, official distributor status, regional warehousing, guaranteed delivery, or customs guarantees.

## Toyota and Hyundai Launch Focus

Toyota and Hyundai makes and models remain an initial browsing structure only. They do not imply published products, approved catalogs, inventory, availability, or quotation readiness.

## Future Expansion Principles

Future expansion can add more brands, categories, internal manufacturing workflows, and stronger SEO content after the RFQ and trust foundation is stable. Expansion should remain B2B, supply-led, and trust-first.
