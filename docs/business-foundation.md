# INCAR Business Foundation

## Positioning

INCAR AUTO PARTS is positioned as a premium China-based automotive supply, sourcing, export, RFQ, and Private Label company for Saudi wholesale auto parts buyers.

Core positioning: Global Automotive Supply & Private Label Solutions from China to Saudi Arabia.

## Target Customer

The primary customer is a Saudi wholesale auto parts buyer, distributor, importer, garage supplier, or purchasing team that needs reliable China sourcing support, RFQ handling, catalog review, packaging coordination, and export preparation.

## Business Model

INCAR supports B2B sourcing from China through:

- Factory sourcing and supplier option comparison.
- RFQ-based wholesale quotation workflows.
- Private Label product and packaging support.
- Quality inspection, packaging review, and sample confirmation support.
- Export coordination for China-to-Saudi supply.
- Catalog and sourcing request conversations that qualify buyer demand.

## RFQ-First Rule

The website should guide serious buyers toward RFQ submission. Products are not retail items for instant purchase; they are starting points for part-number matching, MOQ review, sourcing comparison, and quotation preparation.

## No Ecommerce Checkout Rule

INCAR must not behave like a retail ecommerce store. The site should not add checkout, carts, shopping bags, retail order placement, payment flows, or buy-now behavior.

## No Payment Rule

The website must not collect payment or present payment as a website workflow. Commercial terms should be handled later through direct B2B communication after RFQ review.

## Private Label Core Feature

Private Label is a core business feature, not a small add-on. The site should show that INCAR can help buyers plan product sourcing, factory matching, custom packaging, logo printing, barcode and label design, Arabic/English packaging readiness, sample confirmation, inspection support, and export coordination.

## Catalogs as Lead Generation

Catalogs should operate as a qualified lead path. Buyers request catalogs by sharing company, country, city, WhatsApp, brand, vehicle model or category, and message details. Catalogs may include product photos, part numbers, OEM numbers, MOQ, specifications, compatibility notes, and Private Label availability.

## Trust-First Website Strategy

Every page should answer: why should a Saudi wholesale buyer trust INCAR and send an inquiry?

The trust strategy should emphasize careful service language:

- We help review.
- We coordinate.
- We support.
- We prepare.
- We assist.
- We help compare.

The website should avoid unverified claims such as guaranteed quality, certified factories, lowest price, biggest supplier, official distributor status, Saudi warehousing, guaranteed delivery, or customs guarantees.

## Toyota and Hyundai Launch Focus

The current product and catalog focus should stay on Toyota and Hyundai launch programs. This keeps data quality, buyer expectations, and RFQ flows manageable while the business foundation matures.

## Future Expansion Principles

Future expansion can add more brands, categories, supplier workflows, and stronger SEO content after the RFQ and trust foundation is stable. Expansion should remain B2B, sourcing-led, and trust-first.
