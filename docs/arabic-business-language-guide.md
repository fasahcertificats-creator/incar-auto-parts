# Arabic Business Language Guide

## Language Priority

Arabic is the primary website language for INCAR AUTO PARTS. English remains available as the secondary language for buyers, partners, and internal review.

Default locale: `ar`

Default direction: `rtl`

Secondary locale: `en`

Secondary direction: `ltr`

## Tone

Use professional Gulf business Arabic that feels natural for importers, distributors, workshop suppliers, and wholesale auto parts buyers in Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

The tone should be clear, direct, trust-building, and practical. Avoid academic Arabic, street dialect, exaggerated claims, and literal translation from English.

## Launch Market Focus

Active market messaging must focus on:

- السعودية
- الإمارات
- السعودية والإمارات

INCAR may be described as a global company headquartered in China, but launch-market copy should not expand the offer to all countries, the whole Middle East, or the entire world.

## Forbidden Tone

Do not use casual or exaggerated phrases such as:

- خلنا
- لا تشيل هم
- أبشر
- نعطيك أرخص سعر
- مضمون 100%
- الأفضل في السوق
- أقل سعر
- جودة مضمونة

## No Fake Claims Rule

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed quality
- Certified factories
- Lowest price
- Biggest supplier
- Official distributor
- Warehouse in Saudi Arabia
- Warehouse in UAE
- Guaranteed customs clearance
- Guaranteed delivery

Use credible wording:

- نساعدك في مراجعة المتطلبات
- ننسق مع المصانع
- ندعمك في اختيار المورد المناسب
- نتابع فحص الجودة قبل الشحن
- نساعد في تجهيز بيانات المنتج والتغليف
- نرتب معلومات الطلب قبل عرض السعر
- نركز على احتياجات تجار الجملة في السعودية والإمارات

## RFQ-First Language

INCAR is not a retail ecommerce store. Use RFQ-first terms across the active UI:

- طلب عرض سعر
- طلب تسعير
- إرسال طلب التسعير
- إضافة إلى طلب التسعير
- طلب توريد
- طلب كتالوج

Avoid ecommerce terms such as Buy Now, Add to Cart, Cart, Checkout, Payment, Place Order, and Shopping Bag.

## Private Label Language

Private Label should be presented as a serious B2B sourcing and brand-development workflow. Copy should focus on product category, factory matching, packaging requirements, logo placement, barcode and label readiness, sample review, and export coordination.

Do not imply completed brand projects, guaranteed profits, exclusive factory relationships, or proven client outcomes unless verified proof is added later.

## Trust-Building Copy Rules

Trust copy should explain what INCAR helps review, coordinate, prepare, assist with, and compare. It should not imply guaranteed outcomes.

Good trust copy is specific:

- فحص الجودة قبل الشحن
- التحقق من المصانع
- متابعة التغليف والملصقات
- تجهيز بيانات ومستندات التصدير
- تركيز على السوق السعودي والإماراتي
- قوة التوريد من الصين

Every trust statement should be tied to a practical buyer concern: product fit, part numbers, MOQ, supplier communication, packaging details, labels, export documents, or RFQ follow-up.
