# Private Label Strategy

## Role in INCAR

Private Label is a core business feature for INCAR AUTO PARTS, not a secondary marketing section. It supports the platform position as a premium B2B sourcing, export, RFQ, and brand development partner based in China for Saudi wholesale buyers.

## Target Customer

The primary customer is a Saudi wholesale buyer, importer, distributor, or purchasing team that wants to build an owned auto parts brand through China sourcing while keeping product selection, packaging, quality checks, and export coordination organized.

## Customer Pain Points

- Finding factories that can support the right product category, MOQ, and quality grade.
- Turning a brand idea into packaging, labels, barcode details, and carton marking requirements.
- Avoiding inconsistent supplier communication across product, packaging, and export stages.
- Reviewing samples and packaging details before production.
- Coordinating China export documents and Saudi Arabia shipping requirements.

## Service Scope

INCAR supports:

- Product sourcing.
- Factory matching.
- Custom packaging.
- Logo printing.
- Barcode and label design.
- OEM / ODM support.
- Quality inspection.
- Export documentation.
- Shipping coordination to Saudi Arabia.

The current category scope follows the active product data standard:

- Brake System.
- Suspension Parts.
- Filters.
- Engine Parts.
- Interior Parts.

## Frontend-Only Inquiry Flow

The current `/private-label` inquiry form is frontend-only. It validates buyer details, contact information, product category, estimated quantity, and packaging/message requirements, then shows a mock success message.

No backend, database, authentication, file upload, or request pipeline exists yet.

## Future Brand Builder Direction

Future phases may add:

- Logo file upload.
- Packaging mockup upload.
- Brand Builder workflow.
- Private Label customer dashboard.
- Private Label request pipeline.

These features are documented as direction only and are not implemented in Problem 8.

## No Fake Claims Rule

The page must not claim completed client packaging projects, verified client outcomes, guaranteed profit, exclusive factory relationships, or proven market results unless there is real proof in the project.

## No Ecommerce Transaction Rule

Private Label must remain a B2B inquiry and sourcing workflow. The page must not introduce consumer purchase language, online transaction completion, consumer charging flows, or direct-to-consumer order mechanics.
