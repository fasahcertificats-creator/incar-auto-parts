# Private Label Strategy

## Role in INCAR

Private Label is a core supply capability for INCAR AUTO PARTS, not a secondary marketing section. INCAR remains the customer-facing supplier and commercial counterparty throughout the program.

## Target Customer

The primary customer is a Middle Eastern wholesale buyer, importer, distributor, or purchasing team that wants INCAR to supply a reviewed auto parts program under the customer's brand requirements.

## Customer Pain Points

- Finding factories that can support the right product category, MOQ, and quality grade.
- Turning a brand idea into packaging, labels, barcode details, and carton marking requirements.
- Keeping internal manufacturing communication consistent across product, packaging, and supply stages.
- Reviewing samples and packaging details before production.
- Managing agreed China export-document inputs and target-destination requirements.

## Service Scope

INCAR manages or reviews, according to the approved quotation and order scope:

- Product sourcing.
- Internal manufacturing-source selection.
- Custom packaging.
- Logo printing.
- Barcode and label design.
- OEM / ODM support.
- Quality inspection.
- Export documentation.
- Supply and shipping follow-up for the buyer's Middle Eastern destination.

The current category scope follows the active product data standard:

- Brake System.
- Suspension Parts.
- Filters.
- Engine Parts.
- Interior Parts.

## Frontend-Only Inquiry Flow

The current `/private-label` inquiry form is frontend-only. It can validate fields and prepare an on-device state, but it must not claim that INCAR received the inquiry.

No backend, database, authentication, file upload, or request pipeline exists yet.

## Future Brand Builder Direction

Future phases may add:

- Logo file upload.
- Packaging mockup upload.
- Brand Builder workflow.
- Private Label customer dashboard.
- Private Label request pipeline.

These features are documented as direction only and are not implemented in Problem 8.

## No Fake Claims Rule

The page must not claim completed client packaging projects, verified client outcomes, guaranteed profit, exclusive factory relationships, or proven market results unless there is real proof in the project.

## No Ecommerce Transaction Rule

Private Label must remain a B2B INCAR supply workflow. The page must not introduce consumer purchase language, online transaction completion, consumer charging flows, factory contact choices, or direct-to-consumer order mechanics.
